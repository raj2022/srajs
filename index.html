Searches and analysis

Photons are measured in the electromagnetic calorimeter, which is where the bbγγ search does its work.

05/2024 to present
CMS Collaboration

Lead analyzer, X → YH → bbγγ

Run 3 search for resonant scalar pair production in the NMSSM

I lead a Run 3 search for a heavy resonance X that decays to a Higgs boson, H → γγ, and a new particle Y → bb. It is the successor to the Run 2 analysis.

I built the analysis chain: signal categorization, parametric signal and background modelling, and limit extraction with the CLs method. It runs across a mass grid of about 200 points per data-taking era, with automated multi-year combination and batch jobs on HTCondor.

I am guiding the analysis through CMS review ahead of pre-approval, with a paper to follow.

2020 to 2022
NISER, India

Master's thesis, a neural-network search for T′

Heavy vector-like quark resonances

I built a deep neural network to search for heavy vector-like quark T′ resonances against Standard Model Higgs and non-resonant backgrounds, and benchmarked it against a mature BDT-based CMS analysis using the Higgs Combined Tool.

Earlier projects included Kalman-filter track fitting for an RPC cosmic-ray muon detector and neutrino oscillation phenomenology.

Machine learning

Particle flow combines the tracker, the calorimeters and the muon chambers, so this work touches every layer.

07/2023 to 07/2025
With the MLPF team

Quantization of the MLPF algorithm

Post-training quantization and ONNX deployment

MLPF is a graph neural network that reconstructs every particle in a CMS collision. I developed the post-training quantization and ONNX deployment pipeline that reduces its size and inference latency.

The work is part of the CMS paper on full event interpretation with ML-based particle-flow reconstruction, published in Eur. Phys. J. C.

Quantization, ONNX, graph neural networks, PyTorch

08/2024 to 12/2024
CMS EGamma

Anomaly detection for data quality

An autoencoder that flags bad data, offline and in real time

I developed an autoencoder-based pipeline for offline certification of EGamma data, with an online deployment during Run 3 that flags anomalous events as they are recorded.

I also served as the CMS institutional reviewer for the resulting methodology paper, on Wasserstein-normalized autoencoders.

Autoencoders, anomaly detection, data quality monitoring, real-time deployment

Detector hardware and operations

The pixel detector sits closest to the collision point and records the first measurement of every charged particle.

05/2023 to present
The Catholic University of America

Building forward pixel modules

Graduate researcher, CMS Phase-2 Inner Tracker

I assemble 1×2 and 2×2 forward pixel (TFPX) modules with CROC v2 readout chips for the CMS Phase-2 Inner Tracker upgrade. The university is one of four US CMS production sites, with Purdue, Nebraska and Boston University.

The work covers precision gluing of the high-density interconnect on a gantry system, wire-bonding and pull testing (typical strength about 9 cN), and electrical qualification in a cold box at about −10 °C: IV curves, PixelAlive and Shunt-LDO tests.
